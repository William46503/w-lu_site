02 — My Values

What I stand for

🤝

Honesty & Transparency

I say what I mean and mean what I say. As an ISFJ, I'd rather give someone a clear, honest answer than a comfortable vague one — I think real trust is built on consistency, not just kindness.

🙏

Moral & Gratefulness

I try to do the right thing even when no one's watching, and stay grateful for the people and places — across five languages and three countries — that shaped who I am. Loyalty and follow-through matter a lot to me.

✋

Just a Helping Hand

I notice when people need support, often before they ask for it, and I show up quietly to help — whether that's a friend, a teammate, or a stranger. Taking care of the people around me just comes naturally.
